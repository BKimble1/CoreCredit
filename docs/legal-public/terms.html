These terms describe what CoreCredit does, what it does not do, and what you are responsible for when you use it. They apply to the app as installed on your device.

What CoreCredit is

CoreCredit is a place to write down core charges and keep track of them: what you were billed, which old unit has to go back, which vendor it belongs to, when their return window closes, and whether the credit ever arrived.

It works entirely from the figures and details you enter. It does not read your invoices for you, does not connect to any vendor's system, and knows nothing about your account with a supplier.

What CoreCredit is not

It is not accounting software, and nothing in it is accounting, tax, legal, warranty, or financial advice.

The totals it shows are not books of account and are not a substitute for your own bookkeeping or for professional advice.

It is not a claim system. It sends nothing to any vendor and cannot make a credit happen.

You are responsible for what you enter and what you rely on

Every amount, part number, invoice reference, repair-order reference, vendor, date, and note in the app is the one you or a member of your staff put there.

Text read from a photo, and codes read by the barcode scanner, are convenience features and can be wrong. Check every recognised amount, number, and date against the original paperwork before you rely on it.

Check deadlines, credit amounts, and vendor balances against your invoices and credit memos. If the app and the paperwork disagree, the paperwork wins.

Deadlines and reminders are your own working notes

Return windows and due dates are calculated from the numbers you enter, including the return window you recorded for each vendor.

A vendor can change its policy without telling the app, and a deadline in CoreCredit is your own note of what you believe the policy to be - not the vendor's word.

Reminders are local notifications. They can be late or missed if notifications are turned off for CoreCredit, if the device is off, out of battery, or in a mode that suppresses them. Do not treat a reminder as a guarantee that you will be warned in time.

Vendors and credits

Whether an old core is accepted, and whether a credit is issued and for how much, is entirely between your shop and that supplier, under whatever terms you agreed with them.

CoreCredit has no relationship with your vendors and is not a party to your agreements with them.

The app stores your records on the device, in the app's own storage. There is no account and no developer-operated server, so the developer never receives a copy and cannot recover your records if the device is lost, damaged, replaced, or erased.
